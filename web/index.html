What the numbers describe

DFO groups estimates by population, run and waterbody. One population may have records for several sites. Each line represents one population ID and one waterbody ID. Values from overlapping sites or measures must not be added together.

Blank estimates and years without records leave gaps. A published zero appears at zero. “None observed” describes an inspection and can coexist with a blank numerical estimate. Enumeration methods and survey effort vary over time.
